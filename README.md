# 🛒 Amazon 2.0 - 3D Interactive Freelance Portfolio Showcase

An enterprise-grade, ultra-modern **Amazon Clone** powered by **Three.js WebGL 3D graphics, real-time 360° product inspection, 60fps card tilt physics, and next-gen glassmorphism** created specifically to demonstrate senior frontend engineering capabilities to freelancing clients.

---

## ✨ 3D Innovations That Win Freelance Contracts

### 🌐 Three.js 3D Spatial Arena (Hero Centerpiece)
- **Interactive 3D WebGL Model**: A floating, metallic flagship spatial audio unit with brushed aluminum earcups and glowing LED rings.
- **360° Spherical Orbit Drag**: Click and drag to spin and inspect the gadget from any angle with smooth rotational inertia.
- **Parallax Particle Galaxy**: 450 luminous stars orbiting in 3D space, responding to cursor movements.
- **Interactive Spatial Hotspots**: Pulsing beacon pins that expand live feature callouts on hover.

### 🎴 60fps 3D Perspective Card Tilt Physics
- Every product card dynamically tilts in 3D perspective following mouse coordinates.
- **Dynamic Specular Light Glare**: Realistic light sheen glides across the card surface in real-time.
- **Layered Spatial Depth**: Media, badges, ratings, and buttons lift forward along the Z-axis (`translateZ`).

### 🔬 Quick View 3D Studio 360° Viewer
- Toggle between **"📷 Photo Gallery"** and **"✨ 3D Studio 360°"** in the Quick View modal.
- Inspect the 3D model with **Wireframe mode**, **Lighting presets (Cyber Neon / Studio White / Golden Hour)**, and **Angle Reset**.

### 🛍️ Complete E-Commerce Flow
- **Debounced Live Search Autocomplete** with regex highlight matching.
- **Today's Lightning Deals** with live countdown clock and stock claim progress bars.
- **Slide-Out Cart Drawer** with dynamic Free Shipping progress bar and item removal with **Undo** notification.
- **3-Step Checkout Simulation** with address validation, card preview, confetti celebration, and downloadable receipt.
- **Multi-Currency Converter** ($ USD, € EUR, £ GBP, ₹ INR).
- **Dark Luxury & Classic Light Themes**.
- **Portfolio Specs HUD (`💼 Freelance Specs`)** for instant client conversion.
